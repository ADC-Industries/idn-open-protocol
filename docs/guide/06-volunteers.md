# For volunteers

The screens volunteers use on a Sunday (the video switcher's, the sound desk's, and a
simple mode with only what a service needs) are **the control application**'s. IDN Media is the engine
behind them: it has no switcher or desk screens of its own.

Keep at the desk instead:

- The control application's own guide for the switcher and the desk.
- [Troubleshooting](07-troubleshooting.md) for when a source, the sound or the stream
  goes wrong.
- Who to call, and where the IDN Node panel opens: **IDN Node** in the Start menu.
